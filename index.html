Frequently asked questions

How much warranty do I get on waterproofing?+

It depends on the system — 3–5 years on chemical coating, up to 7 years written warranty on membrane systems.

Can bathroom leakage stop without breaking tiles?+

Yes — in 70% of cases injection grouting and epoxy joint filling fix it without breaking a single tile. Confirmed after inspection.

How is painting charged?+

Interior ₹18–35/sq.ft and exterior ₹25–45/sq.ft including material, depending on wall condition and paint brand. Fixed quotation after free measurement.

How do I pay?+

30% advance, 40% when material arrives, 30% after completion. UPI / bank transfer, with a proper bill.
